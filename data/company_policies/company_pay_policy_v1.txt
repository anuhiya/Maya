PURPOSE AND USE
This policy sets the company's own pay practices at each location. Where this policy and the law differ, the law applies. Questions go to the Payroll Manager.

REMOTE AND HYBRID WORK
Every employee has one office location and one work arrangement: onsite, hybrid or remote. For remote employees, the work location for pay purposes is the approved home address. Hybrid employees who work in two jurisdictions record their primary work location and the second jurisdiction in the payroll system. The company pays a hybrid employee at least the higher of the two minimum wages for all hours worked, so that no hour is underpaid. A change of home address or work arrangement must be approved by the Payroll Manager before it takes effect.
