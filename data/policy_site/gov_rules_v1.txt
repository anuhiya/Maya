PURPOSE AND USE
This bulletin states the payroll rules in force for each jurisdiction. Rules for Avalon appear in sections numbered 1, rules for Calder in sections numbered 2 and rules for Meridian in sections numbered 3. Each rule takes effect on the effective date shown with it. Where a rule shows no effective date, or a date that is not yet set, employers should contact the Division before changing payroll.

WORK LOCATION
Each rule applies according to the jurisdiction where the work is performed, not where the employer's office is or where the employee lives. For an employee who works from home, the home is the work location. For an employee who works in more than one jurisdiction in the same workweek, the employer shall pay each hour worked at no less than the minimum wage of the jurisdiction where that hour is worked.

RULES

1.1 Minimum Wage
Jurisdiction: Avalon
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Every employer shall pay each covered employee a wage of not less than $16.00 per hour for all hours worked.

1.2 Unemployment Insurance Contribution Rate
Jurisdiction: Avalon
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Every employer shall contribute 2.7% of each covered employee's taxable wages to the Avalon Unemployment Insurance Fund.

1.3 Pay Statements
Jurisdiction: Avalon
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Every employer shall furnish each employee, on every regular payday, a written or electronic pay statement showing gross wages, all deductions and net wages.

2.1 Minimum Wage
Jurisdiction: Calder
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Every employer shall pay each covered employee a wage of not less than $14.50 per hour for all hours worked.

2.2 Quarterly Wage Report Filing
Jurisdiction: Calder
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Every employer shall file the quarterly wage and contribution report with the Department within 30 calendar days after the close of each calendar quarter.

2.3 Annual Earnings Report
Jurisdiction: Calder
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Every employer shall report to the Department, once each calendar year, the total earnings paid to and hours worked by each employee.

3.1 Minimum Wage
Jurisdiction: Meridian
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Every employer shall pay each covered employee a wage of not less than $13.75 per hour for all hours worked.

3.2 Overtime Pay
Jurisdiction: Meridian
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Every employer shall pay each non-exempt employee one and one-half times the employee's regular rate of pay for all hours worked in excess of 40 hours in a workweek.

3.3 New Hire Reporting
Jurisdiction: Meridian
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Every employer shall report each newly hired or rehired employee to the Department within 20 calendar days of the employee's first day of work.
