This bill would raise the minimum wage that applies in Los Angeles. If it is enacted, the rule below takes effect on the effective date shown with it. Until then, the minimum wage in the current bulletin stays in force.

RULES

1.1 Minimum Wage
Jurisdiction: Los Angeles
