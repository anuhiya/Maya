Dear Mr. Rios,
On December 29, 2025, the Office of the City Clerk received a draft copy of a proposed
initiative ordinance and request for preparation of an Official Petition Title and Summary
from proponents Rocksand Ramirez, Ana Cecilia Palacios Trujillo, Yajaira Reyes, Vicky
Beza, and Lucero Ramirez Torres. We forwarded the proposed initiative ordinance and
request to the Office of the City Attorney who has prepared and transmitted the Official
Petition Title and Summary set forth below. The City Attorney’s transmittal is limited solely
to that Office’s responsibility to prepare the official petition title and summary and does
not opine on the legality or substance of the proposed measure.
INCREASE TO THE MINIMUM WAGE IN THE CITY OF LOS ANGELES;
CHANGES TO THE CITY’S WAGE AND LABOR LAWS APPLICABLE TO
HOTELS AND CITY AIRPORTS. INITIATIVE ORDINANCE.
The proposed ordinance would increase the minimum wage in the City of
Los Angeles to $25.00 per hour for employers with 26 or more employees and
$20.00 per hour for smaller employers. The minimum wage would increase to
$30.00 per hour by 2030 (by 2032 for smaller employers), and at least 3% annually
thereafter. The ordinance would require that service charges collected from
customers be paid directly to employees. The ordinance would allow waivers by
collective bargaining agreement. The ordinance would restrict future changes to
the City’s wage and labor laws for hotels and airport employers without voter
approval, and make changes to those laws including by: increasing the health
benefit to $8.35 per hour; eliminating certain exemptions/waivers; expanding hotel
worker eligibility for premium pay based on workload; and increasing fines for
violations. The ordinance would prohibit hotels from entering into new contracts
for room cleaning services except in limited circumstances.
AN EQUAL EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY EMPLOYER
This Official Petition Title and Summary is required to appear on the first page of each
petition section above the text of the proposed measure, as provided in City Election Code
Sections 706 and 707. Please contact us if you have any questions regarding the Official
Petition Title and Summary.
The City Election Code requires that you submit a draft copy of your initiative petition,
incorporating the above Official Petition Title and Summary, to this office for review and
approval prior to circulating the petition.
We are sending this letter to you in your capacity as the authorized representative of the
proponents of this initiative ordinance.
For any further questions, please contact us at (213) 978-0444. Thank you.
